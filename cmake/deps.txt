## Format per line:  name;url;hash
##   - hash is SHA256 for release archives (deps.cmake prepends "SHA256=").
##   - an empty hash column means the download is not yet pinned (unverified).
##
## Versions are pinned in the url/hash columns; keep them in sync with
## .github/workflows/windows-build.yml (ONNXRUNTIME_VERSION / LLVM_TAG /
## PROTO_TAG / FLATBUFFERS_TAG).
