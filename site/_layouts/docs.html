Keeping one source of truth (docs_nav in _config.yml) means the sidebar and the pager can never disagree about page order. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} Liquid has no array-push filter, so the lists are accumulated as pipe-delimited strings and split at the end. No label or URL may contain "|". {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign urls_str = "" -%} {%- assign labels_str = "" -%} {%- for entry in site.docs_nav -%} {%- if entry.url -%} {%- assign urls_str = urls_str | append: entry.url | append: "|" -%} {%- assign labels_str = labels_str | append: entry.label | append: "|" -%} {%- endif -%} {%- for child in entry.children -%} {%- assign urls_str = urls_str | append: child.url | append: "|" -%} {%- assign labels_str = labels_str | append: entry.label | append: " · " | append: child.label | append: "|" -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign flat_urls = urls_str | split: "|" -%} {%- assign flat_labels = labels_str | split: "|" -%} {%- comment -%} page.url never carries the baseurl, and neither do the docs_nav entries, so the two are directly comparable. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign here = page.url -%} {%- assign pos = -1 -%} {%- for u in flat_urls -%} {%- if u == here -%}{%- assign pos = forloop.index0 -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
