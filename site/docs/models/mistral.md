Mistral 7B Instruct is the small end of the text side of the matrix. It is a
plain dense decoder with nothing unusual in it, which is the point: it is the
model that leaves the most headroom for everything else on the machine, and
the one to reach for when the question is how the rest of a system behaves
rather than how large a model can be.
